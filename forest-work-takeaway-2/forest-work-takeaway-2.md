# Value over Goals and Rules

In the forest, I don't want to know the goal, I want to understand the value.

If I only know the goal and have rules - the forest has to be cleaned in this fashion - and rules are following,
I miss both understanding and possibility to do the work better (quicker, better result).

Rule can be - stack of branches maximum 2 meters wide with spaces between stacks 6 meters.
I can follow such rules, and the result will be good.

But the value is different: *We need to replant this forest next spring.*
Clear, easy to understand value - the forest was cut down, and we're responsible for replanting it.

From such value, rules and goals naturally emerges!
* Don't do stacks too wide - that would cause spaces in the forest -> next harvest (in 80 years ;-) )
  would produce less usable material
* Don't do stacks too close - not enough space for trees
* Why not burning down those branches? It's suddenly a viable option! Or part of it

This is the way I'd love to do SW.
Give me value, and how is basically up to me. With all consequences like [Bugfixes for free](../bug-fixes-for-free/bug-fixes-for-free.md)
